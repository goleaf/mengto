# Medical Record Master Plan

Date: 2026-08-01

Status: incomplete; 79 medical IDs are verified and 3,788 remain open.

## Contract

The exact Point 7 source is preserved in the combined prompt and contributes
3,867 stable `medical.*` requirements. The combined catalogue contains 29,960
requirements. An atom becomes verified only when implementation, tests,
documentation, and the deterministic evidence overlay agree.

## Phase 64: Foundation, Creation, And Identity

Complete the canonical patient aggregate, one-record invariant, source and
certainty semantics, structured time and units, drafts/import, duplicate
review and merge, identifiers, lifecycle states, summary, and timeline. The
current package establishes the canonical `PetProfile` link, transfer-safe
authorization, and initial knowledge-state model.

## Phase 65: Access And Consent

Complete owner, co-owner, family, sitter, shelter, clinic, specialist,
laboratory, pharmacy, insurer, minor-representative, and disputed-ownership
roles. Add section/purpose/time-scoped capabilities, consent versions,
one-time links, emergency QR, revocation contraction, access logs, break-glass,
and privacy-safe ownership transfer.

## Phase 66: Encounters And Observations

Add canonical encounters, encounter types, reason and history, species-aware
examination, assessment/plan, diagnoses and problem states, chronic episodes,
symptom diary, neutral observation, triage routing, measurements, devices,
quality flags, and comparable trends.

## Phase 67: Allergies And Medication

Normalize allergens, reactions, severity, certainty, intolerance, warning
provenance, active medicines, ingredients, form/concentration, dose units,
route, schedules, PRN instructions, administration log, duplicate-dose guard,
missed doses, reactions, interactions, reconciliation, supply, and supplements.

## Phase 68: Vaccination And Laboratory

Complete vaccine products/components/lots/courses/reactions/exemptions and
minimal-disclosure certificates. Add preventive plans, laboratory orders,
specimens, methods, original units and ranges, corrected versions, critical
result escalation and acknowledgement, trends, genetics, infection, cytology,
and pathology.

## Phase 69: Imaging, Procedures, And Care Plans

Add diagnostic orders and originals, reports, comparisons, annotations and
second-opinion packages. Normalize procedures, surgery, consent, anesthesia,
monitoring, implants, complications, pathology chains, discharge, dental
charts, rehabilitation, multidisciplinary plans, goals, tasks, nutrition,
activity, chronic monitoring, palliation, and quality-of-life observations.

## Phase 70: Emergency, Providers, Documents, And Lifecycle

Complete minimal emergency projections, protected QR and offline copies,
masked contact, travel packages, signed/redacted documents, clinic branches,
professional identity and referrals, teleconsultation and case messaging,
appointments, insurance packages, shelter/foster/adoption continuity, and
respectful memorial transition.

## Phase 71: Security, Data, And Optional AI

Complete append-only clinical revisions, idempotency and conflicts, integrity
validation, protected media delivery, encryption and key lifecycle, MFA,
device sessions, backups, retention, incident response, anomaly detection,
research consent and re-identification review, structured interoperability,
permission-aware search, and draft-only AI with source links.

## Phase 72: Interface, Quality, And Release

Finish EN/LT/RU terminology, protected translation fragments, screen-reader
and keyboard flows, large-text reflow, non-colour states, plain-language
explanations, weak-network and offline behavior, notification escalation,
freshness and duplicate metrics, access contraction tests, medication safety,
and every MVP acceptance gate.

## Phase 73: Scenario Verification

Verify every selected ideal scenario end to end with positive, negative,
privacy, concurrency, migration, rollback, accessibility, mobile, offline,
query-budget, and audit evidence. Scenario text alone is never evidence.

## Global Invariants

- one canonical medical record follows one real pet across ownership changes;
- a social profile never exposes private clinical history;
- unknown, unprovided, suspected, and confirmed facts remain distinguishable;
- original values, units, sources, authors, timezones, and revisions survive;
- current server-side role, purpose, section, expiry, and record state govern
  every read and mutation;
- no AI output becomes a diagnosis, prescription, dose change, certificate, or
  consent decision;
- unfinished Point 7 atoms remain open without optimistic completion claims.
