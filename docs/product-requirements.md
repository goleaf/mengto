# Product Requirements

The following requirements define active functional behaviour. Cross-cutting
fields omitted from an individual row inherit the controls in
`docs/non-functional-requirements.md`.

## Identity And Profiles

| ID | Testable statement | Roles and entities | Authorization and validation | Success / failure | Security, data, i18n, a11y, performance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PRD-IDENTITY-001 | A visitor can register, sign in, sign out, reset a password, and, when the configured mode is enabled, verify an email through server-authoritative flows. | Visitor, member; `User` | Unique normalized email, current password rules, signed expiring links, rate limits; disabled mode stamps the account without claiming independent email-ownership proof | Valid flows rotate session state; enabled mode sends verification mail and gates product access; disabled mode sends no verification mail and enters the portal directly | Store hashed credentials/tokens; keyboard-complete forms; at most one user lookup per attempt |
| PRD-IDENTITY-002 | Every authenticated member has one immutable unique actor key used by existing ownership records. | Member; `User`, owned records | Only the account owner or administrator may change profile data; actor key is not user-editable | Existing `owner_key` records resolve to the correct account; unknown keys deny access | Unique indexed key; never accept an actor key from browser state |
| PRD-IDENTITY-003 | Owner and pet profiles remain separate identities with separate visibility, follows, friendships, and content context. | Member, owner, manager; owner and pet profiles | Portal authentication; owner/manager mutation only; audience values allow-listed | Visible sections render for the selected member audience; hidden sections explain privacy without leaking content | Exact address, medical, microchip, GPS, and private contact data stay private |
| PRD-IDENTITY-004 | A member may manage multiple pets without mixing their care, medical, device, or social records. | Owner, co-owner, sitter; pet profiles | Every operation scopes the selected pet through the authenticated actor's grants | Correct pet data changes; wrong pet or wrong owner returns 403/404 | Pet identifiers are durable and indexed; UI always names the active pet |

## Medical Records And Care Journals

| ID | Testable statement | Roles and entities | Authorization and validation | Success / failure | Security, data, i18n, a11y, performance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PRD-MEDICAL-001 | Each real pet has at most one private canonical medical record that survives ownership transfer. | Owner, co-owner, veterinarian with grant; medical record | Auth, current pet management, unique pet constraint | Record persists with normalized measurements; duplicate rejects; prior owner loses access after transfer | Private by default; critical values auditable |
| PRD-MEDICAL-002 | Medical events, vaccinations, weights, medications, doses, documents, and reminders retain source, time, timezone, verification, and history. | Owner, granted professional; medical entities | Section permissions and validated types/units/times | Structured event persists once; invalid transition rejects | Household vs professional source remains visible; no automatic diagnosis |
| PRD-MEDICAL-003 | Temporary medical access is hashed, scoped, expiring, revocable, and audited. | Owner, veterinarian, temporary recipient; access grant | Owner policy; section and expiry validation | Valid grant reveals only selected sections; invalid/expired/revoked token denies | Never store/log plain token; private download re-authorized |
| PRD-CARE-001 | Every pet has one private, structured daily care journal separated from the medical record. | Owner, co-owner, family, sitter, specialist; care journal | Auth, pet management, unique owner/pet constraint | Correct journal opens; another pet's data never mixes | Private by default; device drafts identify source/confidence |
| PRD-CARE-002 | Structured entries cover feeding, water, walks, toilet, sleep, activity, play, training, behaviour, hygiene, grooming, habitat cleaning, medication, tasks, habits, and observations. | Authorized caregiver; care entry/media | Entry-type permission, enum/unit/time/media validation | Entry records planned/actual values, author, source, status, timezone, and audit | No universal health score or diagnosis; media private |
| PRD-CARE-003 | Feeding records separate planned, offered, consumed, and remaining amounts and prevent accidental double feeding. | Caregiver; feeding entry/task | Portion/unit/product/appetite rules; idempotency and recent-conflict check | One confirmed feed or explicit override; duplicate warns/denies | Source facts preserved when devices disagree |
| PRD-CARE-004 | Walk, toilet, sleep, activity, training, behaviour, and hygiene entries use category-specific structured measurements. | Caregiver, trainer, vet with grant; entries | Category permission and scoped measurement validation | Timeline and period summaries use comparable units | Exact route hidden by default; correlation is not causation |
| PRD-CARE-005 | Routines and tasks support schedules, assignees, priorities, statuses, reminders, handover, and duplicate-safe completion. | Owner, family, sitter, child account; routine/task | Role capability and state transition rules | Completion is idempotent; conflicts show accountable actor | Critical medication tasks require adult capability |
| PRD-CARE-006 | Family and sitter access is individually scoped, time-bounded, revocable, and removed from cached views after expiry. | Owner, family, sitter, professional; access grant | Owner policy; section/action/expiry validation | Recipient sees only granted journal sections and actions | Audit view, mutation, export, location, and expiry |
| PRD-CARE-007 | Daily, weekly, comparison, family, and veterinary summaries cite original entries and never invent missing facts. | Authorized viewer; reports | Report-section policy and period validation | Facts link to sources; absent data is labelled unknown | No automated diagnosis; export private and step-up protected |
| PRD-CARE-008 | Offline or repeated care submissions synchronize without duplicate records and preserve source time and timezone. | Caregiver/device; entry | Client idempotency key, source timestamp, sync status | Retry returns existing entry; conflict remains visible | Do not silently overwrite conflicting source facts |

| PRD-DEVICE-015 | Device and platform AI may summarize, classify, deduplicate, and suggest checks, but cannot diagnose or issue critical commands without confirmation. | Authorized user; AI result/command | Explicit consent, confidence, correction and opt-out | Original evidence remains available; correction is audited | Video/audio processing location, retention, training use, and viewers disclosed |
